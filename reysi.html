About Reysi

Reysi is a Miami-based singer, songwriter, and producer creating cinematic alternative pop and R&B inspired by nightlife, nostalgia, emotional tension, and beach city culture.

Blending alternative pop, indie influences, and Latin rhythm, Reysi’s music is designed to feel immersive, moody, rhythmic, and emotionally honest.

Inspired by artists such as Frank Ocean, Dominic Fike, Finneas, The Weeknd, and Bad Bunny, the project focuses on creating songs that feel like late-night memories people can live inside of.
